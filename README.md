# Paro Varya 🇮🇳

**Document. Discover. Preserve.**

Paro Varya is a community-driven digital platform for documenting and discovering the cultural heritage of communities across India.

It gives people a place to share the heritage around them, from historic places and traditional crafts to festivals, oral histories, local traditions, architecture, food, stories, photographs, interviews, and research.

Instead of treating cultural heritage as something documented only by institutions, Paro Varya explores a community-first approach where **ordinary people can become contributors to the cultural record of their own communities.**

## The Idea

Think of **GitHub for cultural heritage**.

A Heritage Project acts as a collaborative record of a place, tradition, or cultural practice. People can contribute:

- 📷 Photographs
- 🎥 Documentaries and videos
- 🎙️ Interviews and oral histories
- 📄 Research and documents
- 📖 Stories and personal experiences
- 🔗 References and external sources

The goal is to make lesser-known and locally significant heritage easier to **document, discover, and preserve digitally**.

## MVP

The current MVP focuses on four core actions:

**Create → Document → Discover → Contribute**

Users can explore heritage projects, view their locations and documentation, create new projects, and contribute additional material to existing projects.

Paro Varya is currently a prototype. It does not attempt to officially authenticate or certify heritage claims. A future version could introduce community and expert review mechanisms.

## Vision

**Start local. Build for India.**

Paro Varya begins with the idea that meaningful cultural documentation can start with a single person, a single community, and a single story.
